## 🖌️ Design Overview

### 🎨 Color Scheme

- **Primary Color:** #F47C20 (Orange) — energetic and friendly, representing pets’ vibrancy.
- **Secondary Color:** #000000 / #333333 — for text and contrast.
- **Accent Colors:** #FFFFFF for backgrounds and #EAEAEA for soft dividers and highlights.

The color palette emphasizes warmth, trust, and playfulness—qualities associated with pets and their owners.

---

### 🔤 Typography

- **Primary Font:** _Poppins_ — clean, modern, and highly legible.
- **Font Weights Used:** 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (Semi-bold), 700 (Bold)
- **Usage:**
  - Headings: Bold, large sizes for visual hierarchy.
  - Body text: Medium weight for easy readability.
  - Buttons & Links: Semi-bold for emphasis.

---

### 📐 Layout & Structure

- **Navigation Bar:** Simple and fixed at the top for accessibility.
  - Includes links to _Home, Shop, About Us, Contact Us,_ and a _search bar_.
- **Footer:** Consistent across all pages, includes quick links, categories, customer service info, and contact details.

**Grid System:**  
12-column responsive layout ensuring adaptability across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

## 📱 Responsive Design Guidelines

Each layout adapts to:

- **Desktop (≥1024px):** Full layout with sidebars and product grids.
- **Tablet (768–1023px):** Collapsible menus, 2-column grids.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** Single-column layout with stacked content and simplified navigation.

---

## 💡 Design Rationale

The design focuses on:

- **User Friendliness:** Intuitive navigation and logical information hierarchy.
- **Visual Consistency:** Unified colors, typography, and icons across all pages.
- **Accessibility:** Clear contrasts, readable fonts, and simple structure for all users.
- **Engagement:** Use of pet imagery and warm tones to foster emotional connection.
